Global Food Markets 

Food Outlook is a biannual publication focusing on developments in global food and feed markets, published by the Markets and Trade Division of FAO under the Global Information and Early Warning System (GIEWS). Each edition provides comprehensive assessments and short-term forecasts for commodity production, utilization, trade, stocks and prices, as well as feature articles on topical issues. Food Outlook maintains a close synergy with Crop Prospects and Food Situation, produced by the GIEWS, especially regarding cereals.  

DOI:
https://doi.org/10.4060/cd3177en 

Market summaries of the report are forthcoming in Arabic, Chinese, French, Russian and Spanish
