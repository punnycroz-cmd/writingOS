# THE NAME THAT SURVIVED
## CHAPTER 14: SQUAD ALLIANCE (THE MIDPOINT) (FULL NOVEL EDITION)

By 7:00 PM, the storm outside had escalated into a full Atlantic gale. 

Heavy sheets of rain slammed against the curved storm-glass of the lantern room, while the sixty-knot wind howled through the steel guy-wires of the microwave antenna above the tower.

Inside the lantern room, the heavy oak chart table had been transformed into a forward tactical command bridge. Maren had hardwired her field diagnostic rig into the lighthouse’s high-gain microwave transmitter, establishing a high-bandwidth, quantum-encrypted link that bypassed the metropolitan internet grid entirely, routing through an unmonitored transatlantic undersea cable.

Elias sat at the center of the table, wearing a dual-channel headset with a noise-canceling boom mic. 

He keyed the private, peer-to-peer Layer-3 frequency.

Three resonant chimes sounded in rapid succession through his earphones, followed by the familiar, comforting clatter of mechanical keyboards and charging virtual capacitors.

"Vane!" Viper’s booming, raspy voice erupted over the channel, rattling the diaphragms of the headset. "Where the hell are you?! Every major news network on the East Coast is running your face on a red banner! They’re saying you took a hostage and burned down an entire medical wing!"

"I'm at the Point Lookout relay," Elias spoke into the mic, his voice calm, steady, and stripped of all deception. "Listen to me, all of you. What the news is saying is a corporate cover-up. Twenty years ago, Dr. Iris Halloway conducted an illegal neural experimentation program on an orphan cohort at an off-grid research facility. When a lead biochemist named Elena Solis tried to rescue the children, Halloway murdered her, burned the facility to the ground, and used an early prototype of the dream-layer technology to rewrite my brain."

A heavy, suffocating silence descended over the voice channel.

"She planted a false memory of guilt," Elias continued, his gray eyes reflecting the amber glow of the monitors. "She made me believe I started that fire. She kept me broken, docile, and isolated for twenty years so she could use my neural pathways to unlock Layer 4. And now, she has deployed an armed private tactical squad to execute me and Elena Solis’s daughter—Dr. Maren Solis."

"Jesus Christ..." Ghost_Kev breathed, his fingers stopping on his keyboard. "That’s not just corporate malpractice, Elias. That’s a state-sponsored black-budget military program. If Halloway perfects Layer-4 cognitive synchronization, she won't just control patients—she’ll have the power to rewrite the autobiographical identity of entire populations."

"We have the decrypted raw incident file right here on this drive," Elias said. "Tonight at midnight, Maren and I are going to initiate an offensive breach against Halloway’s master Level-0 command server. We are going to extract the uncorrupted archive, route it through this microwave transmitter, and broadcast it to the FBI, the Department of Justice, and every major international press agency simultaneously."

"Elias, that Level-0 server is protected by Halloway’s private cyber-tactical division," Cipher_Lin said, her voice sharp with urgent technical reality. "It’s a hardened Layer-3 fortress. They have corporate operators with Level-4 administrator privileges, automated security sentinels, and active data-eraser payloads. You cannot solo a military fortress, Vane. If they corner your avatar in Layer 3, their admin counter-measures will trigger a fatal neuro-vascular feedback loop in your physical brain."

"I know the risks, Lin," Elias said quietly. "I'm going in anyway. Because if we don't end this tonight, Maren and I will spend the rest of our lives running through the dark until they put a bullet in our heads."

For five long seconds, the only sound on the channel was the howling of the wind outside the lighthouse.

And then, a heavy mechanical *CLACK* echoed through the line as Viper toggled his avatar’s status from *Idle* to *Combat Ready*.

"He won't be going in alone," Viper growled, his rough voice vibrating with an ancient, unyielding loyalty. "We’ve run five hundred raids together, Vane. We bled through three winter championship seasons in the dirt. You think we're going to sit on our asses in our bedrooms while some corporate butchers try to fry our point-man?"

"Viper's right," Ghost_Kev said, the rhythmic, high-speed clicking of his keyboard resuming with lethal precision. "I'm re-routing my tactical sniper rig through an offshore proxy in Reykjavik. I'll provide long-range fire support on their perimeter defense towers."

"I’m compiling an active firewall-penetration worm right now," Cipher_Lin added, her voice fierce and determined. "I’ll run point on the server's data vaults and keep their admin lockouts suppressed. Give us the IP handshake, Elias. We're dropping in with you."

Elias looked up from his screen.

Across the oak table, Maren was watching him, her dark eyes wide with an astonishment that bordered on reverence. She had spent her entire adult life in the polished, cynical world of corporate medical boards and forensic courts, where every alliance was transactional and every signature was bound by a non-disclosure agreement. 

She had never seen a brotherhood like this—strangers who had never met in the physical world, pledging their lives and their minds to fight an empire for a friend who had lost his past.

Elias swallowed, a lump of emotion rising in his throat.

"Handshake transmitted," Elias whispered into the mic. "Midnight, squad. We take their fortress down."

"Copy that, boss," Viper grinned over the comms. "Lock and load."
