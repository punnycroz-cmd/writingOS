# THE NAME THAT SURVIVED: English edition notes

This is the English edition of *Cái Tên Còn Sống Sót*, Book 1 (v2, 36 chapters). It is written as native English prose rather than a line-by-line translation. Plot, scene order, and dialogue beats follow `novel_vi_v2/`, and `CANON_V2_CAI_TEN_CON_SONG_SOT.md` still governs facts.

## Voice
- Close third person, one POV per section, same as the Vietnamese.
- Cinematic and emotionally direct. Characters are allowed to feel things on the page.
- Sentences run long when a scene is calm or a character is thinking, and get short when something breaks. Avoid strings of fragments for "punch."
- Squad scenes stay warm and funny. Let the jokes land without explaining them.
- The knock code (three quick, two slow) and the lullaby are kept for key moments only.

## Things to avoid
- Em dashes in narration. Keep them mostly for cut-off dialogue ("LENA—").
- "It wasn't X. It was Y." frames, and other "not X but Y" constructions.
- Tidy groups of three in every paragraph.
- Abstract filler such as *the weight of*, *a testament to*, *something shifted*, *a beat*, *the air was thick with*.
- Explaining a feeling right after showing it.

## Names and address
- **Elias Vane.** Linh calls him "Eli." Viper calls him "Vane" or "kid." Halloway calls him "Zero."
- **Lena Hale / Maren Solis.**
  - Before Ch.33, narration calls her Lena.
  - From the moment she says "My name is Maren," narration calls her Maren.
  - Ruth and Corwin keep calling her Lena.
- **Lin / Linh.**
  - Before Ch.12, narration says "Lin" (her handle, Cipher_Lin).
  - From Ch.12 on, narration says "Linh."
  - In dialogue, Elias, Kev, and Viper say "Lin," and Lena/Maren says "Linh."
- **Viper** is Marcus Reyes. **Kev** is Kevin Tran (Ghost_Kev). Viper's handle is Viper_99.
- **Vietnamese pronoun shift (tôi → tớ/cậu at Ch.23).** English cannot mark this with pronouns. Mark it instead through register:
  - Before Ch.23, Lena and Elias are careful with each other and use full names and professional distance.
  - From Ch.23, in private they talk like two kids through a wall: plain, teasing, no titles.
  - Save "Eli" from Maren's mouth for Ch.33.
