# THE NAME THAT SURVIVED
## CHAPTER 20: DIVE PROTOCOL (FULL NOVEL EDITION)

At 11:45 PM, the roar of the approaching combat drone engines began to bleed through the howling gale outside—a sinister, low-frequency electrical drone that made the glass panes of the lantern room vibrate in their bronze channels.

Elias settled his tall frame into the reclining leather chair beside the oak chart table. 

His face was pale, carved from stone, his storm-gray eyes utterly tranquil in the face of impending annihilation.

Maren stood over him, her movements swift, precise, and imbued with a fierce, protective focus. She fitted the curved titanium sensor band across his forehead, adjusting the tension knob until the twelve micro-transceivers and six cryogenic contact pads locked against his skull with a series of muffled, mechanical clicks.

She took his left arm, swabbed the inner crook of his elbow with alcohol, and slid a high-flow intravenous cannula into his median cubital vein, securing the line to a pressurized bag of Neuro-Vascular Stabilizer Alpha-9 hanging from an IV pole beside the chair.

"The IV line will automatically administer continuous micro-boluses of stabilizer based on your intracranial telemetry," Maren said, her voice steady, though her dark eyes were filled with unshed tears. "The moment you breach the Level-0 Core, the system will signal my terminal, and I will execute the global broadcast script."

She paused, her hand resting firmly against his shoulder, her fingers gripping the fabric of his shirt as if she could physically hold him to this world.

"Elias, look at me."

Elias opened his eyes, looking up into her face.

"If you feel your brain slipping toward the event horizon of Layer 4... you pull out," she demanded, her voice cracking with fierce, desperate emotion. "You do not cross that threshold, Elias. Your vascular walls are damaged. If you enter Layer 4 again without a quantum regenerator pod, your brain will suffer a catastrophic hemorrhagic stroke before you can even take a breath. Promise me you will stay in Layer 3."

Elias reached up with his right hand, covering her fingers where they rested on his shoulder, squeezing gently.

"I promise you, Maren," he whispered, his thumb lightly stroking the back of her hand. "I will stay in Layer 3. We'll take their data, and I will come right back to you."

He paused, his gray eyes softening with an infinite, tender warmth. "Watch my back out here."

Maren swallowed, leaning down until her forehead lightly touched his for a single, fleeting heartbeat.

"Always," she whispered against his skin.

Elias closed his eyes.

Maren stepped back to the master console, took a deep breath, and slammed the primary power breaker into position.

The quantum synthesizer roared to life with a deafening, high-pitched turbine whine that drowned out the storm outside. The amber monitors cascaded with rivers of green code:

> **`[NEURAL SYNCHRONIZATION ENGAGED: LAYER 3 PROTOCOL]`**  
> **`[BANDWIDTH: 100 GB/S VIA HIGH-GAIN MICROWAVE BEAM]`**  
> **`[TARGET HOST: HALLOWAY LEVEL-0 DEFENSE FORTRESS]`**  
> **`[CONSCIOUSNESS THRESHOLD BREACHED: ZERO SECOND LATENCY]`**

Elias’s body went completely limp against the leather headrest as his conscious mind was torn from the physical world, plunging headfirst into the digital abyss.
