# THE NAME THAT SURVIVED
## CHAPTER 24: THE HACKER’S GAMBIT (FULL NOVEL EDITION)

Back in the physical world, the iron security gate at the top of the lantern room staircase buckled beneath a sledgehammer blow.

Four black-clad tactical operators in heavy ballistic armor, tactical respirators, and night-vision goggles surged through the breach, their suppressed carbines snapping upward to sweep the lantern room.

Maren fired from behind the oak chart table.

*THWUMP.*

A high-velocity, fifty-thousand-volt stun-cartridge struck the lead operator squarely in the center of his chestplate. The massive electrical discharge arced through his nervous system, seizing his muscles in violent tetany and dropping him like a sack of wet concrete onto the landing. 

The remaining three operators immediately opened fire. 

Suppressed 9mm automatic rounds tore through the charts, chipping sharp shards of granite from the wall beside Maren’s head and showering her hair in white stone dust.

Outside the panoramic curved windows, the three heavy combat drones hovered twenty feet from the glass, their six-barreled miniguns spinning up with a menacing, high-pitched electrical whine, targeting the interior of the room.

Maren ducked behind the table, clutching her rifle, knowing that a single three-second burst from those miniguns would liquefy the room and everything inside it.

*BEEP.*

On the desk beside her, Maren’s secondary diagnostic display abruptly went pitch-black.

A single command-line prompt in blinding neon cyan flashed onto the center of the dark screen:

> **`[INTRUSION DETECTED: ZERO-KNOWLEDGE DARK-NET PROXY]`**  
> **`[UNKNOWN_USER]: "Need a hand with the pest control, Doctor?"`**

Before Maren’s fingers could even reach the keyboard, a blinding flash of electric blue light erupted outside the curved storm-glass.

The navigation strobes on all three hovering combat drones flipped instantaneously from hostile crimson to cool, electric blue.

The three gunships pivoted one hundred and eighty degrees in mid-air with impossible, robotic synchronicity.

Their miniguns roared.

A devastating, synchronized hail of armor-piercing depleted-uranium rounds rained down from the sky, chewing into the four black armored transports parked in the courtyard below. Engine blocks were shattered, tires were shredded, and within four seconds, the pressurized fuel cells detonated in colossal, towering fireballs of orange and black smoke that illuminated the entire promontory.

Down in the stairwell, the three surviving operators screamed as their integrated night-vision respirators short-circuited with a violent shower of white sparks. 

The electronic smart-triggers on their carbines locked solid as the unseen Hacker flooded their squad’s encrypted tactical subnet with a catastrophic, weaponized denial-of-service payload.

"What the hell happened to our comms?!" an operator bellowed in the dark, throwing his dead weapon down the iron stairs. "Our weapons are bricked! Fall back to the perimeter!"

The operators scrambled down the spiral staircase in blind, terrified retreat.

On the chart table, the laptop speaker crackled to life, broadcasting a smooth, digitally modulated female voice:

*"Exterior perimeter neutralized, Doctor. I’ve hijacked their drone telemetry and broadcast an emergency priority beacon to the regional Federal SWAT division—they’re inbound by helicopter, ETA four minutes."*

The Hacker paused, her voice dropping into an urgent, lethal seriousness:

*"But check your biometric telemetry, Maren. Your boy inside the Level-0 server is redlining. His intracranial pressure is off the charts. If you don't get him out of that server in ninety seconds, he's going to die in that chair."*
