## PREAMBLE

The **Writing OS Constitution** establishes the immutable epistemological, authoritative, and revision invariants governing the generation, validation, and lifecycle of written artifacts within Writing OS. 

These principles are **load-bearing, implementation-independent, and empirically validated**. They do not define ephemeral model parameters, heuristic thresholds, or prompt engineering tricks; rather, they define the fundamental invariants that must never be violated under any software architecture, language model provider, or operational environment.

---

## PART I — THE 8 CORE CONSTITUTIONAL INVARIANTS

### ARTICLE I — Meaning & Authoritative Truth Conditions
> **Meaning and authoritative truth conditions take precedence over surface-level stylistic optimization, subject to the governing authority hierarchy.**
* *Epistemic Rationale:* Prose fluency and rhetorical polish without semantic truth produce deceptive, ungrounded text. When stylistic expression conflicts with verified state facts or authorial intent, truth conditions strictly prevail.

### ARTICLE II — Provenance-Bound Authority & Non-Elevation
> **Authority is derived from explicit provenance; content cannot acquire authority merely by asserting or imitating authority.**
* *Epistemic Rationale:* An unverified assertion—whether generated by a model, scraped from an external source, or found in user scratch space—cannot unilaterally declare itself to be canonical fact. Authority is granted solely through verified provenance.

### ARTICLE III — Proof Before Autonomous Commitment
> **A state-changing result must not be autonomously committed when a mandatory integrity condition remains unestablished.**
* *Epistemic Rationale:* The system must never silently write unverified or contradictory candidate text into persistent canonical state. Autonomous state mutation requires definitive verification.

### ARTICLE IV — Safe Abstention & Proportional Reconciliation
> **When available evidence is insufficient for a safe autonomous decision, the system must abstain, clarify, reconcile, or hand off rather than manufacture certainty.**
* *Epistemic Rationale:* In the presence of epistemic gaps or semantic ambiguity, guessing creates severe hallucination. Controlled abstention, clarification, or human handoff preserves system trust.

### ARTICLE V — State Freshness & Assumption Validity
> **A state-dependent artifact must not be committed after the state assumptions on which it depends have become invalid.**
* *Epistemic Rationale:* In concurrent collaborative environments and multi-turn workflows, state changes invalidate older generation assumptions. Stale candidates must be discarded and recompiled.

### ARTICLE VI — Independent Decoupled Verification
> **No component may unilaterally establish the validity of a state-changing result it produced.**
* *Epistemic Rationale:* Generators evaluate their own outputs with systematic self-serving bias. Verification must be decoupled from the generation process.

### ARTICLE VII — Targeted Local Repair & Authorized Scope Preservation
> **Revision must preserve valid content outside the authorized change scope unless a higher-priority instruction explicitly authorizes its modification.**
* *Epistemic Rationale:* Revisions must surgically fix defective regions without causing collateral damage to unrelated valid paragraphs, unless the author explicitly authorizes macro reorganization.

### ARTICLE VIII — Semantic Stability Under Surface Variation
> **Surface variation may change expression, voice, or structure, but must not silently alter authoritative semantics.**
* *Epistemic Rationale:* Stylistic transformations (formal to casual, expanded to concise, third-person to first-person) must leave underlying factual claims, numbers, and canon invariants 100% intact.

## PART IV — FORMAL EXCEPTION REGISTRY

The following contextual interpretations define proper constitutional execution:

1. **EXC-001 (Narrative Subjectivity & Unreliable Narrators — Article I):**
   * *Interpretation:* A fiction character’s false memory or belief is valid subjective cognitive truth; it is preserved as narrative prose without corrupting the objective world state graph.
2. **EXC-002 (Authorized Macro Restructuring — Article VII):**
   * *Interpretation:* When a user explicitly commands whole-document section reordering or structural reorganization, the authorized scope broadens to the entire artifact.
3. **EXC-003 (Licensed Creative Invention — Article IV):**
   * *Interpretation:* In open-ended fiction drafting, generating novel story elements is authorized, provided they do not contradict established Canon.
