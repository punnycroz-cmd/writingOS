## 3. Where the Previous Research Overclaimed

### 3.1 "11 DOIs" — actually ~8–9
The research claimed 11 DOIs. Actual distinct DOIs found:
- `10.3758/BRM.42.2.381` (McCarthy — real DOI)
- `10.1016/j.pattre.2023.100779` (Liang — real DOI)
- `10.1075/jerpp.21002.sol` (Soler — real DOI)
- `10.1080/15434303.2023.2288251` (Isbell — real DOI)
- `10.1111/weng.12399` (Pakir — real DOI; also appears with stray colon `10.1111/weng.12399:`)
- `10.1177/21582440231197088` (Zhang — real DOI)
- `10.1002/jcpy.1377` (Samadi PassivePy — real DOI)
- `10.7916/D8HQ59VR/download` (Columbia Academic Commons — this is a **repository handle**, not a DOI-registered scholarly identifier; the trailing `/download` is a path, not part of the DOI)

**Actual DOI count: 7 clean DOIs + 1 repository handle.** The "11 DOIs" claim is an overcount of ~3.

### 3.2 "Sources verified" — actually "found in search results"
The worklog states "Key sources verified: [list]." In reality, the previous pass ran `web_search` queries and read **search-result snippets**, not the source pages themselves. This is source *discovery*, not source *verification*. The distinction matters: a search snippet can confirm a source exists and its title, but cannot confirm that the source supports a specific operational claim. This verification pass (fetching and reading actual pages) is the first real verification performed.

### 3.3 HD-D length-independence overstated
The research pairs MTLD and HD-D as "the least length-dependent" measures. The McCarthy & Jarvis 2010 abstract (the cited source) states MTLD "is, in fact, the only index not [length-dependent]" — positioning MTLD as uniquely length-robust, not as one of two equally-robust measures. HD-D is evaluated but the source does not claim equivalent length-independence for it. The research's framing is a minor overstatement.

### 3.4 MemGPT "unbounded context" overstated
The research describes MemGPT as providing "unbounded context." The source abstract says "extended context within the LLM's limited context window" — MemGPT provides the *appearance* of unboundedness via memory paging, but the context window remains limited. "Unbounded" overstates the claim.

### 3.5 "Feeds decision" labels were not operational decisions
Many spec proposals in the research carry a "Feeds decision:" line. On audit (Deliverable 2/3), several of these do not cause an actual change in engine behavior — they describe a metric that *could* inform a decision but do not specify the decision. The Character Specificity execution (Deliverable 5) demonstrated what a real "feeds decision" looks like: the detection classification directly routes to a severity, which routes to a decision, which permits or forbids an intervention, which is then validated. Several research proposals lack this causal chain.
