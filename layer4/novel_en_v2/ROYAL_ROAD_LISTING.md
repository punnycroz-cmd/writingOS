# THE NAME THAT SURVIVED: Royal Road listing kit

Draft listing for posting Book 1 (English v2) as a web serial. The tag names below could not be checked against Royal Road from this environment, so match them against the site's dropdowns when you create the fiction.

---

## Title
**The Name That Survived**

## One-line hook
He forgot his whole life. He kept one name.

## Blurb (full)

Elias Vane wakes from an eleven-day coma with thirty years of somebody else's life burning out of his head.

Somewhere in Layer 4, the deepest level of the Somnia dream network, where time runs a thousand times faster, his mind came apart, and strangers' memories poured in to fill the gaps. Now he has no past, no family, and a hospital chart that calls him Subject Zero. The hospital lets him walk out the front door. A black car follows him home.

Only one thing survived: a name. *Maren.* And the face of a nine-year-old girl screaming through smoke.

The face belongs to Lena Hale, a memory forensics expert who has never heard the name Maren in her life. Her fiancé disappeared at sea eight months ago while investigating Halloway Neuro-Systems, the company behind Somnia and the AI companions three hundred million people talk to every night. His notebook has Elias's name in it.

With a sniper who never sleeps, a hacker who builds useless bubble machines, and a single dad who drives a taco truck, they have six weeks to work out what was done to two children twenty years ago.

The woman who did it has been waiting for them to find each other.

*A neural sci-fi thriller about stolen memories, found family, and a promise made through a wall.*

## Blurb (short, for social posts or cross-promotion)

A man wakes from a coma with no memory and one name. A memory examiner has never heard that name, but her body reacts to it anyway. Twenty years ago, someone made sure they'd forget each other. Now three hundred million AI companions are about to open the door to everyone's memories.

## Launch plan (suggestion)
- **Launch day:** chapters 1–3. Ch.1 is the hook (dream life + "allow subject to move freely"), Ch.2 lands the first payoff ("I'm back"), and Ch.3 introduces the squad and Nyx. Three chapters give a reader enough to follow.
- **Then:** a steady schedule you can actually keep, for example three chapters a week (Mon/Wed/Fri). 36 chapters at that pace run about 11 weeks.
- **Chapter length:** about 1,500–2,800 words, which is normal for the platform. No splitting is needed.
- **If you plan a Kindle Unlimited release later:** KDP Select requires the ebook to be exclusive to Amazon. Serial authors usually remove or "stub" the Royal Road chapters before the KU launch, so plan your announcement around that.
