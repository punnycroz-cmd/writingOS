# THE NAME THAT SURVIVED
## CHAPTER 22: REAL-WORLD BREACH (FULL NOVEL EDITION)

The first thermal breaching charge detonated against the iron-reinforced storm door at the base of the lighthouse with a bone-jarring *CRACK* that rattled the teeth in Maren’s jaw and shook the dust from the cedar rafters eighty feet above.

Outside, the rain had turned into a slashing squall. Four matte-black tactical transports idled in an aggressive semi-circle on the promontory, their high-intensity xenon searchlights cutting through the driving mist to pin the lantern room in harsh, blinding beams of white light.

"Step away from the control console and place your hands behind your head!" a voice boomed through an acoustic Long-Range Audio Device mounted atop the lead transport, its sound waves rattling the glass panes. "Dr. Solis, you are harboring an armed federal fugitive. Surrender the physical storage drive, and you will be granted full corporate immunity under protective custody."

Maren knelt behind the heavy oak chart table, her pneumatic stun-carbine resting across her knee.

"Go to hell," she whispered through gritted teeth.

She glanced over her shoulder. 

In the reclining leather chair, Elias sat motionless, his head supported by the curved headrest, his eyes closed beneath the glowing titanium sensor band. The digital cardiac monitor beside the chair showed his heart rate climbing in jagged, erratic spikes: *142 beats per minute.* His left temple was pulsing with a violent, rhythmic twitch that pulled the skin taut across his cheekbone.

*He was deep inside their perimeter.*

*BOOM.*

A second, heavier shaped-charge detonated against the lower iron door. 

The smell of vaporized steel and burning thermite drafted up the open spiral stairwell. Through the floorboards, the agonizing screech of warping iron hinges echoed like the death cry of a wounded beast.

Heavy, armored boots hit the concrete floor of the lower bunker.

"Stairwell clear!" a synthesized voice barked through a tactical squad radio below. "Move up! Target is on the top deck!"

Maren took a slow, deliberate breath. She stood up, stepped to the top landing of the spiral staircase, and raised the stun-carbine to her shoulder, aligning the glowing green holographic reticle with the curved iron steps fifteen feet below.

"Hold the line, Elias," she whispered into her headset mic, her finger tightening on the smooth curved trigger. "Just hold the line."
