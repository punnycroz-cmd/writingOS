# THE NAME THAT SURVIVED
## CHAPTER 26: THE CONFESSION (FULL NOVEL EDITION)

In the physical world, the cardiac monitor went completely, terrifyingly insane.

*BEEP-BEEP-BEEP-BEEP-BEEP.*

The digital display on the telemetry workstation flashed in violent, pulsating banners of blood-red:

> **`[CRITICAL BIOMETRIC REDLINE: MULTI-ORGAN FAILURE IMMINENT]`**  
> **`[HEART RATE: 188 BPM — VENTRICULAR FIBRILLATION DETECTED]`**  
> **`[INTRACRANIAL PRESSURE: CRITICAL FATAL THRESHOLD]`**  
> **`[NEURAL SYNCHRONIZATION: LAYER 4 COGNITIVE DRIFT ENGAGED]`**

On the reclining leather chair, Elias’s body arched violently off the cushions. 

His jaw was locked so hard that a trickle of bloody saliva leaked from the corner of his lips. His fingers were frozen in rigid, clawed spasms against the armrests, and from both of his nostrils, thick, dark streams of arterial blood poured down his chin, soaking the collar of his navy coat.

Maren dropped her stun-carbine to the floor with a clattering thud.

"ELIAS!" 

She lunged across the lantern room, slamming both of her palms against the glass partition of the workstation console, her voice cracking with pure, unadulterated terror.

"Elias, STOP!" she screamed into the comms headset, tears instantly flooding her eyes. "Do not open Layer 4! You're tearing your brain apart! You're going to kill yourself!"

Through the distorted, static-choked audio feed of his neuro-link, she heard his voice—faint, fading, floating in the terrifying distance of a dying consciousness:

*“It’s... okay, Maren...”* he whispered, every syllable costing him a lifetime of pain. *“If I... if I shatter their core... you will be safe... You will have your justice... You will be free...”*

*He was dying for her.*

In that shattering, terrifying second, every single defense mechanism Maren had constructed over twenty years of grief dissolved into nothingness. 

She didn't care about the Black File. She didn't care about Iris Halloway. She didn't care about proving her mother's innocence, and she didn't care about the corporate empire that had stolen her youth.

None of it mattered. The only thing in the entire universe that mattered was the man bleeding to death in that chair.

Maren pounded her fists against the glass, hot tears pouring down her face, her chest heaving as she screamed with the raw, primal fury of a woman refusing to let death take her soulmate:

"NO, ELIAS! LISTEN TO ME!" she cried, her voice tearing from the very bottom of her soul. "I DON'T CARE ABOUT THE FILE! I don't just see you as a patient, or an ally, or a friend! I love you, Elias! Do you hear me?! I LOVE YOU! You are not allowed to die in that dark! You have to fight! YOU HAVE TO COME BACK TO ME!"
