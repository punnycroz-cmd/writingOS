# WRITING OS — GOVERNANCE & AMENDMENT PROTOCOL

**Document Status:** Official Governance Charter  
**Foundational Authority:** Constitution v1.0.0 (Part III & Part V)  

---

## 1. DEDICATED GOVERNANCE CHARTERS

### CHARTER A: Register & Tenant Isolation Charter
* Register-specific policies (Fiction, Academic, Business, Technical, Legal, General) must remain modularly encapsulated in isolated packs.
* Multi-tenant data namespaces, operational session contexts, and permission filters must maintain absolute non-intersecting isolation.

### CHARTER B: Evaluation & Metrics Governance Charter
* External measurement systems, commercial benchmarks, and third-party AI detectors (e.g. GPTZero) are observational research signals.
* No external metric may directly acquire constitutional authority or unilaterally alter core generation policies without independent validation.

---

## 2. THE HIERARCHY OF CHANGE

Modifications to Writing OS must strictly flow upward through empirical validation:

$$\text{Data / Telemetry} \longrightarrow \text{Evidence Card} \longrightarrow \text{Policy Registry} \longrightarrow \text{Operating Principle} \longrightarrow \text{Constitution}$$

* **Policy Updates:** Can be updated dynamically via version-tracked policy profiles.
* **Operating Principle Updates:** Require documented evidence and register pack validation.
* **Constitutional Amendments:** Require formal 4-stage ratification: Evidence Card Registration $\to$ Counterevidence Audit $\to$ Adversarial Stress Testing $\to$ Explicit Ratification.

---

## 3. NON-GOVERNING FIXTURES

* **`final.txt`:** Formally designated as an observational **Fiction Stress Fixture**. It does not constitute a quality gate, release criterion, or constitutional requirement for Writing OS.
